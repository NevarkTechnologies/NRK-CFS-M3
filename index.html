Campus Food Ordering

Nevark's BenchBites is a campus food-ordering application developed by Nevark LLP. The application allows students and staff to browse canteen menus, place orders, make online payments, track orders in real time, and collect food using a digital token. BenchBites is intended for use within educational institutions, including universities and colleges.

What it is

An ordering counter that fits in your pocket

Nevark's BenchBites connects students and staff with the canteens on their own campus. You browse each canteen's live menu, add items to a cart, and place the order from your phone or browser. Payment is handled online through our payment partners, and you receive a token-coded order that the canteen verifies when you collect your food.

Because the order is placed and paid for in advance, the kitchen starts preparing it before you arrive. You follow the status in real time and get a notification the moment it is ready — no waiting at the counter during a short break between classes.

How it works

Three steps, one break

Pick your Canteen

Open the live menu for any canteen on your campus. Items and prices are set by the vendor, and sold-out dishes disappear as they go.

Order and Pay

Build your cart and pay online. You get a token-coded receipt and a pickup window straight away.

Collect and Go

Track the status in real time, walk over when it's ready, and show the token at the counter.

Features

Built for the ten minutes you actually have

Live Canteen Menus

Every vendor manages their own items, pricing and availability, so what you see is what's actually cooking.

Online Payment

Pay by UPI, card or wallet through our payment partners. No cash, no change, no counter fumbling.

Token Pickup

Each order carries a token the canteen verifies at handover, so nobody collects the wrong order.

Real-time Status

Watch the order move from accepted to preparing to ready, with a notification at each stage.

History and Reorder

Past orders stay in your account, so your usual is two taps away tomorrow.

Vendor Dashboard

Canteens get their own view to manage menus, accept incoming orders and see the day's totals.

Your data

Why we use Google Sign-In

Google Sign-In is the only sign-in method in Nevark's BenchBites, so you never create or remember another password. When you sign in we request the basic profile scopes only:

  • Name — shown on your profile and passed to the canteen so they can identify your order at pickup.
  • Email address — used as your unique account identifier and for order receipts.
  • Profile picture — displayed on your profile screen inside the app.

We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, Contacts, Calendar or any other Google service. We never sell Google user data or share it with third parties for advertising. Data received from Google APIs is used solely to provide the ordering features described on this page.
